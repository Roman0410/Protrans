Our processes

01

Request analysis

We communicate with the client, determine cargo specifications, deadlines, budget, and special requirements. We prepare several solution options.

02

Route planning

We select the most efficient delivery method based on deadlines, cargo type, and budget. We create the optimal route and prepare all necessary documents.
